Implementasi Teknologi AI dalam Promosi UMKM: Program Pemberdayaan Komunitas Orang Tua SD Kanisius Kadirojo

A publication co-authored by Flourensia Sapty Rahayu, Rangga Perwiratama, and Sion Felix Saragih.

Overview

This community program helped home-based small businesses build digital and AI skills for promotion. Participants practiced using Google Gemini for copywriting, Canva AI for promotional visuals, Google Business Profile, and AI video tools. The article reports that over 70% of respondents said their practical skills improved.

Published: 30 May 2026 · Journal: GIAT Teknologi untuk Masyarakat, Vol. 5, No. 1.
