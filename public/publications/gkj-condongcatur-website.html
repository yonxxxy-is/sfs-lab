Pengembangan Website dalam Meningkatkan Akses Informasi dan Kegiatan Jemaat Gereja Kristen Jawa Condongcatur

A publication co-authored by Sion Felix Saragih, Fanidyasani Atantya, Moch Alif Budi Setyawan, and Rangga Perwiratama.

Overview

The project aimed to make the GKJ Condongcatur website easier to use and maintain. The team gathered requirements through interviews and surveys, redesigned the site responsively, developed it with Laravel, and trained church staff to manage its content.

Published: 1 July 2025 · Proceedings: Prosiding Seminar Nasional KONSTELASI, Vol. 2, No. 1 (2025).
